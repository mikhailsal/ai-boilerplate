## Limitations

- The full merge gate did not complete on this host at `4d597a7`; the
  hosted run is the regression of record, and it ran the lanes as two jobs
  over the extension's commits from `dfb2322`, where the local gate runs
  both over every commit from `main`.
- The hosted run was a `pull_request` event; `merge_group` and `push`
  events, the ruleset, and the GitLab wrapper were not observed.
- The slots bound how many whole-gate tests share the CPUs, not how fast
  each runs; every scratch command keeps its 900-second limit, so a much
  slower host can still time out.
- The audit judges the advisories known on the day it runs.
